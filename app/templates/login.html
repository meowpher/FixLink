Student Notice: This document constitutes a legally binding institutional agreement governing your access to FixLink, your responsibilities regarding campus infrastructure and smart classroom equipment preservation, maintenance reporting protocols, and your data protection rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.
Article 1: Institutional Scope, Jurisdiction & Binding Agreement

1.1 Statutory Authority & Assent: By authenticating, accessing, or utilizing FixLink, you, as an enrolled student of Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University (MIT-WPU), Pune, enter into a binding legal contract under the Indian Contract Act (1872), the Information Technology Act (2000), and institutional bylaws. If you do not agree to these terms in their entirety, you must immediately discontinue use of the platform.

1.2 Student Eligibility & Enrollment Standing: Access to FixLink is granted strictly to students with active academic enrollment. Graduation, academic withdrawal, administrative suspension, or de-registration immediately and automatically terminates platform authorization.

1.3 Scope of FixLink Facilities: The platform facilitates real-time reporting of physical infrastructure defects, status observation of smart classroom assets across the Vyas Building and adjacent academic blocks, and authenticated communication with certified university technicians and facility coordinators.

Article 2: Student Credential Custody & SSO Authentication Policy

2.1 Mandatory Institutional Single Sign-On (SSO): Students must access FixLink exclusively through their designated institutional account (PRN@mitwpu.edu.in or name.prn@mitwpu.edu.in). Personal or non-university email domains are prohibited for student authentication.

2.2 Anti-Impersonation & No-Proxy Mandate: Account credentials, passwords, session tokens, and one-time verification links are strictly personal and non-transferable. Submitting maintenance requests, messaging technicians, or logging in on behalf of another student is treated as identity fraud under university disciplinary codes. The registered student associated with the PRN remains strictly responsible for all actions executed under their session.

2.3 Public & Shared Terminal Security: When accessing FixLink from public kiosks, laboratory workstations, or shared classroom podiums, students must explicitly log out and terminate their browser session to prevent unauthorized access.

2.4 Immediate Compromise Reporting: Any suspected unauthorized access, credential leakage, or session hijacking must be reported within twelve (12) hours to the IT Security Helpdesk at it-security@mitwpu.edu.in.

Article 3: Smart Classroom, Laboratory & Campus Infrastructure Care

3.1 Duty of Preservation: Students must exercise diligence and care when utilizing smart classroom infrastructure, including 4K interactive touch panels, digital projection hardware, ceiling audio arrays, wireless microphones, laboratory computing units, HVAC controllers, sensory IoT nodes, and electrical distribution switches.

3.2 Strict Prohibition of Vandalism & Hardware Tampering: Intentionally disconnecting display cables, tampering with smart classroom touch interfaces, damaging HDMI/Type-C wall plates, stealing computer peripherals (mice, keyboards, power adapters), scratching furniture, defacing acoustic wall panels, or tampering with electrical circuit breakers constitutes actionable institutional property damage.

3.3 Hazardous Substances & Beverage Restrictions: Bringing unsealed liquid containers, open food items, or unauthorized chemical agents into smart classrooms or computer laboratories is strictly prohibited to prevent accidental equipment ruin.

3.4 Immediate Hazard Reporting: Students discovering broken electrical sockets, exposed live wiring, water leakage, structural damage, or malfunctioning fire safety fixtures are obligated to raise an immediate priority ticket via FixLink to protect campus community safety.

Article 4: Maintenance Work Order Protocols & SLA Expectations

4.1 Authenticity & Physical Verifiability: Every ticket submitted via FixLink must reflect a genuine, physical fault. Attaching fraudulent photos, misleading descriptions, satirical content, or unrelated memes is strictly prohibited.

4.2 Accurate Categorization: Users must classify defects under the appropriate maintenance discipline (Electrical, Plumbing, Audio-Visual, Computing Hardware, HVAC, Furniture & Fixtures, Civil/Structural).

4.3 Priority & Severity Integrity: Minor aesthetic blemishes must not be tagged as "High Priority" or "Emergency Hazard". Falsifying urgency impairs the triage response for life-safety emergencies.

4.4 Professional Decorum in Communications: Interactions with certified technicians, engineering personnel, and administrative dispatchers within the ticket messaging system must remain professional, courteous, and objective. Harassment, abusive language, or obstruction of maintenance staff will result in immediate disciplinary escalation.

Article 5: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Compliance

5.1 Data Fiduciary Notice: Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University acts as the Data Fiduciary. By logging in, students provide informed consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act (2023) for processing essential service data.

5.2 Categories of Processed Data: The platform processes your Full Name, Permanent Registration Number (PRN), Institutional Email, Academic Program, IP Address, Device Fingerprint, and Timestamped Ticket Logs.

5.3 Purpose Limitation & Zero Commercial Monetization: Student data is utilized strictly for campus facility maintenance, infrastructure quality improvement, and safety enforcement. Personal data is never sold, licensed, or shared with commercial advertising entities.

5.4 Storage & Encryption Standards: Telemetry and ticket data are stored on secure university servers within the Republic of India and encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

5.5 Data Principal Rights & Grievance Redressal: In accordance with statutory provisions, students have the right to request access to their telemetry logs, seek rectification of inaccurate information, or lodge grievances with the University Data Protection Officer at dpo@mitwpu.edu.in.

5.6 Cookie Minimization & Incognito Operation: FixLink utilizes exclusively first-party, strictly essential session cookies for authentication. When accessing the platform in Incognito or Private Browsing mode, session cookies are held in ephemeral browser memory and are automatically destroyed upon closing the private window, leaving zero persistent tracking cookies on your device.

Article 6: Cybersecurity, Anti-Tampering & CERT-In Compliance

6.1 Prohibition of Circumvention: Students shall not utilize automated bots, scrapers, script injection, developer console manipulation, or unauthorized API fuzzing to bypass terms acceptance gates, spam maintenance queues, or access unauthorized administrative endpoints.

6.2 Immutable Forensic Audit Logs: In compliance with Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) directives, all network interactions, IP addresses, user agent headers, and clickwrap timestamps are permanently logged and retained for forensic audits.

Article 7: Disciplinary Sanctions, Financial Restitution & Appeals

7.1 Disciplinary Escalation: Violations of these terms, fraudulent ticketing, physical asset vandalism, or cyber-tampering will be referred directly to the MIT-WPU Proctorial Board and Student Disciplinary Committee for disciplinary action, up to and including digital suspension or academic debarment.

7.2 Monetary Restitution: Any student found liable for deliberate damage to campus electronics, smart boards, or laboratory computing equipment shall be required to pay the full replacement and installation cost, charged directly to their university student ledger.

7.3 Student Ombudsman & Inquiries: Inquiries, legitimate feedback, or maintenance queries may be addressed to the Central Facilities Helpdesk at facilities@mitwpu.edu.in or the Student Affairs Office.

8.1 Automated Processing & Data Variance: You expressly acknowledge that FixLink relies on automated software routines, heuristic room occupancy detection, asynchronous database sync, and network telemetry. Faculty & Administration Notice: This section contains the mandatory Ghost Protocol attendance accountability policy, ad-hoc space reservation regulations, priority maintenance workflows, and administrative compliance governance for MIT-WPU Pune.
Article 1: Institutional Authority, Scope & Executive Mandate

1.1 Statutory Authority: Enacted under executive direction of the Vice-Chancellor and Registrar of Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University (MIT-WPU), Pune, this governance framework regulates space management, smart classroom technology, and infrastructure maintenance across all faculty and administrative divisions.

1.2 Role-Based Administrative Scope: Faculty members, Department Heads (HODs), Central Maintenance Managers (CMM), and Super Administrators are granted specific role-based permissions. Exercising permissions outside authorized academic scope is strictly prohibited.

Article 2: Faculty Credential Integrity & Anti-Delegation

2.1 Official Credential Use: Faculty and staff must authenticate exclusively via their designated university email (@mitwpu.edu.in).

2.2 Anti-Delegation Mandate: Delegating administrative credentials, sharing login passwords, or authorizing student proxies to reserve rooms or modify master schedules under faculty credentials constitutes a violation of university administrative protocol.

Article 3: The Ghost Protocol — Smart Space Utilization & Attendance Enforcement

To eliminate unoccupied classroom reservations, guarantee equitable resource distribution, and maintain zero-waste operational efficiency across the Vyas Building, FixLink enforces the automated Ghost Protocol engine:

3.1 15-Minute Grace Period: For all ad-hoc room reservations, special seminars, and departmental events, the reserving faculty organizer or designated co-instructor must physically occupy the room and record an authenticated digital check-in within exactly fifteen (15) minutes of the scheduled slot start time.

3.2 Automated No-Show Cancellation & Space Reallocation: If an authenticated check-in is not logged before the expiration of the 15-minute grace period, the FixLink engine automatically revokes the reservation, immediately returns the room to the active campus vacancy pool for other faculty, and records an immutable No-Show Strike on the organizer's profile.

3.3 30-Day Rolling Window & 14-Day Suspension Enforcement: Accumulating three (3) active strikes within any rolling 30-day window automatically triggers a mandatory fourteen (14) calendar day suspension of ad-hoc room booking privileges.

3.4 Master Timetable Protection: Scheduled academic curriculum timetable lectures assigned by the Central Timetable Committee are protected and will not be auto-cancelled by ad-hoc strike suspensions.

3.5 Prohibition of Room Squatting: Occupying smart classrooms, laboratories, or seminar halls without an active timetable slot or an approved FixLink booking is prohibited.

Article 4: Ad-Hoc Room Reservation Rules & Event Management

4.1 Advance Booking Window: Ad-hoc reservations may be placed between a minimum of two (2) hours and a maximum of fourteen (14) calendar days prior to the required time slot.

4.2 Good-Faith Advance Cancellation: If a reserved classroom is no longer required, the organizer must cancel the booking at least one (1) hour prior to slot commencement. Timely cancellation frees the space and incurs zero penalty or strike.

4.3 Conflict Resolution Hierarchy: Conflicting space requests are resolved by strict operational priority: (1) University Examinations > (2) Master Timetable Lectures > (3) Approved Institutional Conferences > (4) Departmental Seminars > (5) Ad-Hoc Sessions.

Article 5: Priority Maintenance Work Orders & Emergency Escalations

5.1 Priority Dispatch Authority: Faculty members possess authorization to flag active instructional defects (e.g. smart panel failure during ongoing lecture, projector power failure) as "High Priority" for fast-track technician dispatch.

5.2 Critical Safety Hazards: Suspected electrical short-circuits, structural anomalies, chemical spills, or fire equipment faults must be immediately flagged for full physical evacuation and urgent administrative dispatch.

5.3 Repair Verification: Faculty organizers must confirm the physical rectification of reported faults prior to approving ticket closure.

Article 6: DPDP Act (2023) Compliance, Telemetry & Confidentiality

6.1 Student Data Protection: Faculty and staff accessing student ticketing data, PRN identifiers, or maintenance histories must handle all records with strict confidentiality under the DPDP Act (2023).

6.2 Prohibition of Unauthorized Data Extraction: Bulk downloading, exporting, or sharing of campus facility reports, technician metrics, or student telemetry with external non-university entities is prohibited.

Article 7: Immutable Cryptographic Audit Trails & Forensics

7.1 Unalterable Action Logging: All room reservations, cancellations, check-in verifications, strike issuances, administrative waivers, and work order modifications generate tamper-evident cryptographic log entries.

7.2 CERT-In Compliance: Forensic server logs, client network timestamps, and authenticated user states are retained in accordance with national cybersecurity directives.

Article 8: Strike Appeals, Administrative Disputes & Amendments

8.1 48-Hour Dispute Window: Faculty members wishing to contest an automated strike due to verifiable technical anomalies, network outages, or urgent departmental summons may submit a formal appeal to Central Administration within forty-eight (48) hours of strike notification.

8.2 Valid Grounds for Waiver: Documented campus network failure, medical emergencies, or official Dean/HOD convocations constitute acceptable grounds for strike mitigation.

8.3 Institutional Amendments: MIT-WPU reserves the right to revise operational protocols in alignment with university academic calendars and facility governance guidelines.

Article 9: Algorithmic Telemetry Disclaimer, "As Is" Operation & Developer Immunity

9.1 Algorithmic & Automated Space Telemetry Disclaimer: The automated Ghost Protocol engine, check-in countdown timers, occupancy tracking heuristics, and live vacancy calculations operate through automated system logic. Because network synchronization, campus Wi-Fi handover, or server latency can occasionally cause temporary data lag, FixLink does not guarantee that room status telemetry is 100% infallible or real-time under all environmental conditions. Faculty organizers are advised to conduct independent verification for university examinations, guest symposiums, or high-stakes institutional events.

9.2 "As Is" Provision & Non-Liability: FixLink and its scheduling modules are deployed on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime, absolute accuracy, or fault tolerance.

9.3 Absolute Developer, Maintainer & Engineering Team Personal Shield: Faculty members, departmental coordinators, and administrative officers expressly acknowledge and agree that the software engineers, student developers, maintainers, and individual system architects who built and maintain FixLink act purely in a technical software development capacity. Under no legal theory (contractual, tortious, statutory, administrative, or common law) shall the individual software developers or maintainers be held personally liable, sued, disciplined, prosecuted, or subject to financial, civil, or employment claims for any software bugs, unexpected booking cancellations, strike generation errors, timetable collisions, platform outages, or data loss.

9.4 Waiver of Claims & Hold Harmless: By authenticating and accepting this agreement, faculty and administrative users irrevocably waive all claims, causes of action, and legal remedies against the individual developers and maintainers of FixLink, agreeing to hold them completely harmless from any operational consequence or institutional dispute arising from platform utilization.
