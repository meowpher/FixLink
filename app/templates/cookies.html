Cookie, Local Storage & Incognito Policy

Operational Scope: Vyas Smart-Room Tracker & FixLink Campus Platform | Last Updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Browser Storage Technologies?

Cookies and browser storage mechanisms (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) are compact data structures placed temporarily or persistently on your client device by web servers. They serve strictly essential operational functions: verifying user identity across requests, upholding authentication security, defending against automated request tampering, and saving client-side display preferences.

FixLink adheres strictly to the Data Minimization Principle mandated under Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. The platform does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral analytics beacons.

2. Essential Storage Key Inventory

FixLink utilizes only the following first-party, strictly necessary cookies and client-side storage keys. No cookie or storage key is deployed for any purpose other than those enumerated below:

3. Incognito & Private Browsing Operation

How Cookies Function When You Sign In Under Incognito / Private Mode

When you access FixLink in Incognito Mode (Google Chrome / Brave), InPrivate (Microsoft Edge), or Private Browsing (Mozilla Firefox / Apple Safari), the browser allocates a temporary, sandboxed memory space for your session:

  • Session Authentication: When you log in, FixLink sets the strictly necessary session cookie in your browser's isolated incognito RAM. This is technically mandatory for the server to recognize your authenticated user account as you navigate between pages.
  • Zero Persistent Storage: FixLink does not write persistent cookies to your physical hard drive during Incognito browsing.
  • Immediate Automatic Destruction: The instant you close your Incognito window or private tab, your web browser automatically and permanently destroys the session cookie, CSRF token, and any temporary local storage entries. No login artifact remains on the physical device.
  • No Super-Cookies or Device Fingerprinting: FixLink does not employ browser canvas fingerprinting, audio API sniffing, hardware UUID harvesting, or cross-session tracking to identify you once your private window is closed.

4. Absolute Prohibition of Third-Party Commercial Tracking

Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University does not partner with commercial advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing profiling services. FixLink does not load:

  • Third-party behavioral tracking cookies (e.g. Meta Pixel, Google AdSense, Criteo)
  • Cross-site retargeting scripts or social media tracking widgets
  • Commercial user data brokers or monetization trackers

5. Complete Logout & Cookie Purge Protocol

When you click Sign Out or access /logout:

  1. The server-side session dictionary is immediately invalidated and cleared (session.clear()).
  2. The server transmits explicit HTTP deletion response headers (Set-Cookie: session=; Max-Age=0; Expires=Thu, 01 Jan 1970 00:00:00 GMT; Path=/) commanding your browser to immediately purge the session cookie from active storage.
  3. Any subsequent request to protected campus routes is immediately blocked and redirected to the login gate.

6. Managing Your Browser Cookie Settings

You possess full autonomy to inspect, block, or delete cookies via your browser's native privacy settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies > Clear browsing data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings > Safari > Advanced > Privacy > Block All Cookies / Remove All Website Data.
Notice on Disabling Strictly Necessary Cookies: If you completely block all first-party session cookies in your browser settings, you will be unable to log in, report maintenance defects, or view smart room occupancy schedules.

7. Inquiries & Data Protection Officer (DPO)

For statutory questions regarding data protection, telemetry practices, or cookie governance under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023), please contact the University Data Protection Officer at dpo@mitwpu.edu.in or Central Facilities Management at facilities@mitwpu.edu.in.
