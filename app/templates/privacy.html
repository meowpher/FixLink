Privacy Policy & Data Processing Notice

Draft Date: September 2026

1. Purpose & Scope

FixLink is an internal facility operations platform deployed by MIT World Peace University ("MIT-WPU") to track, manage, and dispatch repairs for smart classroom equipment, laboratories, and physical campus infrastructure. This Privacy Policy informs students, faculty, and administrative staff regarding how personal data is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Data Minimization Principles

In alignment with data protection and privacy-by-design standards, FixLink enforces strict data minimization. We only collect personal attributes directly necessary to facilitate issue verification and maintenance workflows:

  • Full Name: Utilized for reporter identification and job communication.
  • Permanent Registration Number (PRN) / University ID: Employed to verify active enrollment or faculty status.
  • University Email Address: Used strictly for automated ticket status updates, completion notices, and authentication.
  • Report Data: Facility issue category, classroom location, timestamp, and optional issue photographs.

FixLink does not collect payment details, national identification numbers, personal phone numbers from students, or persistent geolocation tracking data.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing & Consent

Personal data is processed pursuant to legitimate campus administrative interests and explicit user consent provided at the time of ticket submission. By checking the consent verification box on our reporting forms, users acknowledge and consent to the collection of their PRN and email solely for the purpose of dispatching and verifying the requested facility maintenance.

4. Access Controls & Disclosure

Access to reported tickets and reporter contact details is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis:

  • Assigned Technicians & Professionals: View room locations, fault descriptions, and reporter names for on-site task completion.
  • Facility System Administrators: Oversee campus maintenance resolution metrics and ensure quality of service.
  • Third Parties: FixLink does not integrate any third-party advertising or analytics services as of this version. User data is not sold or shared with external marketing brokers.

5. Data Retention & Archival

Maintenance records and associated reporter metadata (PRN and university email) are retained indefinitely in the active database. FixLink currently operates with no automated deletion or archival schedule (operational metrics utilize active analytical windows of 60 days for asset health depreciation and up to 180 days for trend reporting, while raw ticket histories remain stored permanently for equipment warranty and campus audit logs unless manually expunged by system administrators).
