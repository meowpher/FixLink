Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Reviewed: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and client-side storage technologies (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) are small text files or key-value entries stored on your computer or mobile device when you access web services. They are widely used to maintain secure logins, preserve user interface preferences, and facilitate application performance.

2. How FixLink Uses Cookies

FixLink follows a privacy-conscious implementation and limits cookies strictly to operational requirements. We categorize our storage mechanisms as follows:

Category Cookie / Storage Key Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary session Maintains secure user login state and server session. Session (expires on logout or browser close)
Security csrf_token Guards form submissions against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). Session
Preferences theme (localStorage) Preserves user preference between Light Theme and Dark Theme. Persistent (client-side only)
Consent cookie_consent Records user choice regarding non-essential diagnostics or telemetry. 12 months

3. Consent & Tracking Audit Controls

Under our data minimization policy, external analytics and third-party tracking scripts do not execute by default. They are strictly gated behind explicit consent (cookie_consent). When consent is absent or refused, no third-party tracking cookies are deployed or read.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow users to manage cookie settings, including blocking all cookies, blocking third-party cookies, or wiping cookies upon exiting the browser. You may adjust these settings through your browser's preferences:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Please note: Disabling strictly necessary session cookies will prevent login authentication and ticket submission functionality.
