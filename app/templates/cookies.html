Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Draft Date: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and browser storage technologies (such as localStorage) are small text files or key-value entries stored on your device when you use web applications. They are used to keep you signed in, preserve interface preferences, and support application security.

2. How FixLink Uses Cookies

FixLink uses the following cookies and storage keys. No cookie is set for a purpose other than those listed below.

Category Key / Mechanism Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary session (HTTP Cookie) Maintains authenticated user session state and account verification on the server. 24 hours (86,400 seconds) or until logout
Security csrf_token (Hidden form field / session value) Protects form submissions against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). Transmitted via form inputs and request headers; not stored as an independent browser cookie. Active 24-hour session duration
Preferences theme (localStorage, client-only) Stores your selected visual theme (Light Theme or Dark Theme) locally on your device. Never sent to the server. Persistent until browser storage is cleared
Consent cookie_consent (Template gate) Gating flag evaluated in application templates before loading optional telemetry. Not set as a persistent cookie in the current release. None / Not set (0 days) — no tracking scripts are active

3. Your Choices About Non-Essential Cookies

External analytics and tracking scripts do not run by default. They only execute if non-essential cookie consent is explicitly enabled. If consent is absent or declined, no third-party tracking scripts or cookies are loaded.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage, inspect, or delete cookies through your web browser's privacy settings. Consult your browser vendor's documentation for detailed instructions.

Note: Disabling the strictly necessary session cookie will prevent login authentication and ticket submission.
