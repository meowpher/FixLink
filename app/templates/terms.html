Terms of Service & Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Reviewed: September 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or submitting information through the FixLink platform ("Service"), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service, along with applicable MIT World Peace University ("MIT-WPU") IT policies and student code of conduct regulations.

2. Authorized Users & Access

Access to FixLink is provided exclusively to registered students, faculty members, facility administrative personnel, and authorized technical staff of MIT-WPU. Users must access the platform using their officially assigned university credentials and must not impersonate other members of the academic community.

3. Acceptable Use & Reporting Integrity

FixLink is engineered solely for bona fide reporting of classroom hardware defects, electrical faults, laboratory anomalies, HVAC malfunctions, and general university maintenance needs. Users agree NOT to:

  • Submit false, frivolous, malicious, or fabricated maintenance reports.
  • Upload inappropriate, abusive, defamatory, or non-work-related media attachments.
  • Attempt to circumvent platform security, session management, or role authorization boundaries.
  • Harass, intimidate, or post defamatory reviews regarding university technicians and maintenance staff.

4. Ticket Triage & Resolution Service Levels

Submission of a fault report does not guarantee immediate repair. Tickets are automatically categorized, prioritized, and dispatched according to severity (e.g., electrical hazards and exam hall emergencies take precedence over minor cosmetic repairs). Campus administration reserves the unilateral right to reprioritize, reassign, consolidate, or close duplicate reports.

5. Equipment Liability & Disclaimer

FixLink functions as a coordination and workflow dispatch platform. MIT-WPU Facility Operations strives for maximum equipment reliability but disclaims any warranty regarding instantaneous turnaround times. Users must report urgent electrical or life-safety emergencies directly to campus security.

6. Modifications & Termination

The university reserves the right to modify these operational terms or suspend platform access for individuals who repeatedly violate acceptable use policies. Continued usage following notice of revisions constitutes acceptance of amended terms.
